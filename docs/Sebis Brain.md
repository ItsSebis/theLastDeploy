## Design
> The overall design ofcourse stays VS Code based, but when the game seems tireding there needs to be some creative enchancing effect

## Survival
- Companion difference, payment and Support abilities (take one focus to trigger)
    - Senior dev ($5/day)
        - Quit when high tech dept and not very good relationship
        - When good relationship offers Support from time to time to lower the tech dept back down to zero (think of other possibilities)
    - PM ($4/day)
        - Quit when reputation is low and not very good relationship 
        - Support (again needs good relationship and is offered from time to time): pitches you to a big corp boosting reputation
    - Designer ($3/day)
        - Brainstorm quitting reasons
        - Brainstorm a support ability too
    - Intern ($1/day)
        - Quit after 30 days or when you speak too much with them
        - Offers no payment when money is low and on a very rare basis can score a "my uncle works at microsoft" (or similar) to boost reputation very high and gives a money boost
    - QA Ghost (buy warning for 7 days for $15, no automatic payment)
        - Does not quit
        - Offers to be hired for $1/day when very good relationship
        - Support ability: Guards you from incidents fully for a night
